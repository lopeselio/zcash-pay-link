How it works

        
    
          
  • The link uses the ZIP-321 standard. Opening it, or scanning the QR code, opens the payer’s Zcash wallet with your details filled in. They check and send it there.
    • 
          
  • Nothing you type is sent anywhere. This page has no server, no analytics and makes no network requests; your setup lives only in the address bar.
    • 
          
  • It only needs your receiving address, never a seed phrase or private key. Don’t paste one here or anywhere else.
    • 
          
  • Prefer a shielded address (Unified or Sapling). Payments to transparent addresses can be seen by anyone.
